Our Underwater Services

At Southeast Diving Services, we don’t just clean boats — we tune the underwater performance of your vessel so it runs smoother, faster, and more efficiently. Our experienced commercial dive teams bring decades of marine expertise straight to your hull, propeller, and running gear. Serving private boat owners, marinas, and commercial operators throughout South Florida, our diver-operated services are available by appointment or emergency call-out.

Hull & Bottom Cleaning

A clean hull is a high-performing hull. Marine growth like barnacles, algae, and slime cause drag, reduce fuel efficiency, and can damage coatings over time. Our professional divers use proven techniques to remove soft and hard growth from your vessel’s underwater surfaces — all without hauling your boat out of the water. Regular bottom cleaning keeps your boat moving smoothly while protecting the integrity of your hull paint and anti-foul coatings.

Running Gear Services

Your propeller, shafts, trim tabs, and thru-hull fittings play a huge role in performance. We clean and inspect all running gear elements so they operate at peak efficiency. Removing barnacles, sea grass, and debris helps improve handling, reduce vibration, and extend the life of essential components.

Zinc (Anode) Replacement & Corrosion Protection

Sacrificial anodes (zincs) protect your boat’s metal components from corrosion in a marine environment. Our divers inspect and replace zincs on-site to ensure your boat remains shielded from damaging electrolysis. Proper anode maintenance increases the lifespan of underwater metalwork and reduces costly repairs.

Underwater Inspections & Reports

Our dive teams provide thorough underwater inspections, documenting the condition of your hull, running gear, and underwater hardware. We deliver clear before-and-after visuals and detailed reports so you know exactly what was done and how your vessel’s underwater condition looks.

Propeller & Running Gear Removal

Skip hauling your boat — our divers can safely remove and reinstall propellers and other running gear while your vessel remains in the water. This service helps save time, money, and hassle while keeping you on the water longer.

Scheduled Maintenance Plans

Consistent underwater cleaning and inspection extends the life of your boat and helps prevent expensive repairs. Ask about our customized maintenance plans built around your schedule, usage, and local marine growth conditions. Regular service is one of the best ways to protect your investment and enjoy trouble-free time on the water.
