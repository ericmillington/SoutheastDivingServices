Ship, Dock & Seawall Repair

Structural Work Where It Actually Matters—Below the Waterline

Damage below the waterline doesn’t fix itself. When docks, seawalls, pilings, or vessel structures start failing underwater, it’s only a matter of time before small issues turn into big, expensive problems. Our commercial dive teams handle underwater ship, dock, and seawall repairs safely, efficiently, and without unnecessary haul-outs or disruption to your operation.

If it’s underwater and structural, we’re built for it.

Underwater Ship & Vessel Repairs

Keep Working Vessels Working

From minor structural fixes to underwater hardware support, we assist with in-water repairs and inspections for commercial vessels, workboats, and ships. Our divers work alongside marine contractors and shipyards to perform underwater tasks that would otherwise require costly dry-docking.

Services include:

  • Underwater inspections and condition assessments
  • Assistance with structural repairs below the waterline
  • Support for hull penetrations, fittings, and hardware
  • Debris and obstruction removal
  • Pre- and post-repair inspections for verification

We help you stay operational while protecting the integrity of your vessel.

Dock Repair & Maintenance

Protect the Structure That Protects Your Boat

Docks take constant abuse from water movement, corrosion, marine growth, and vessel impact. Our dive teams inspect and repair underwater dock components to keep your marina, private dock, or commercial facility safe and functional.

Dock services include:

  • Inspection of underwater dock structures and hardware
  • Piling inspections and stabilization support
  • Underwater fastener and bracket repairs
  • Debris removal around dock structures
  • Support for marine contractors during dock repairs and upgrades

Preventative dock maintenance keeps your waterfront safe and avoids costly structural failures.

Seawall Inspection & Repair Support

Stop Erosion Before It Starts Taking Your Property With It

Seawalls protect shoreline property from erosion, collapse, and storm damage—but most failures begin below the waterline where you can’t see them. Our divers provide underwater inspections and repair support to identify compromised areas early and assist contractors with targeted repairs.

Seawall services include:

  • Underwater seawall inspections
  • Identification of erosion, voids, and structural weaknesses
  • Support for underwater repairs and stabilization
  • Debris removal and access support for marine contractors
  • Pre- and post-repair condition reporting

Early detection saves shoreline property—and serious money.

Why Underwater Repair Matters

Ignoring underwater damage leads to:

  • Structural weakening
  • Accelerated corrosion
  • Dock instability
  • Seawall collapse and shoreline erosion
  • Increased liability and safety risks

Underwater inspection and repair catches problems early—before they become emergencies.
