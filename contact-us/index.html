We Dive. That’s What We Do.

Day or night. Dockside or offshore. Our commercial dive team is available 24/7/365 for underwater boat cleaning, hull maintenance, propeller inspections, zinc replacement, and emergency dive services throughout Miami-Dade, Broward County, and the Palm Beaches.

When it’s below the surface and needs to be done right, we’re already geared up.

If you’re looking for high-quality dive services, you have come to the right place. At Southeast Diving Services we provide personal service and attention to detail. We offer superior full-service underwater vessel maintenance and monthly cleanings.

Southeast Diving Services (S.E.D.) is licensed and insured and has been located in Fort Lauderdale since 1989. We service Dade, Broward, and Palm Beach counties daily and provide dive teams at any destination upon request. Please contact our main office.
