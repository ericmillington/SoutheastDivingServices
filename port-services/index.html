Port Services

Certified Port Contractor

Bowie Balentine serves as our Port Manager. He recruits, trains, and leads our skilled dive teams, providing the motivation and leadership that have been crucial to the success of our port operations and underwater maintenance services.

We support a wide range of marine infrastructure projects, including:

  • Ports and terminals
  • Marinas and waterfront facilities
  • Commercial docks and piers
  • Bulkheads and seawalls
  • Marine construction and repair projects

Our dive teams are trained to work in challenging underwater environments and coordinate with marine contractors, engineers, and project managers.
